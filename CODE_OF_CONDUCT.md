# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good faith, critique ideas and code rather than people, and make space for differing experience levels and perspectives.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information. Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the Wavect maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

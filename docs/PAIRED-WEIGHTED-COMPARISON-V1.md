# Paired weighted comparison v1

Status: focused local source, native and normal-compiler checks passed; hosted gates pending.

Audience: compiler contributors and reviewers.

The ordinary ActiveBuilder caller may compare the two actual observation
languages synchronously after their existing construction and admission. The
lexical caller still completes its terminal census, arithmetic check and
Enumerated preflight before this comparison. Counts and representation headers
do not establish equality. Selected and independent Oracle payers retain their
existing route.

The comparison computes a postorder of all nodes reachable from both roots,
rejecting unknown targets and cycles. An epsilon frontier sums checked positive
u64 weights in reverse postorder, so every predecessor contributes before its
target is visited. Labelled derivatives are sorted by the complete existing
Label order and target index; equal label/target movements add their checked
weights. The two accepted weights and all label derivatives must match. A
visited pair contains complete immutable weighted numeric states, compared by
value. Postorder strictly decreases across a derivative, so memoization never
turns a cycle into proof. Sharing a state does not grant equality authority.

Only complete success returns positive evidence. Missing labels, multiplicity
mismatch, overflow, malformed topology or the optimization's finite state
boundary retain the complete preceding canonical comparator. A physical or
work refusal propagates through the original ByteFailure/Diagnostic boundary;
it does not resume computation or clear the first refusal. The ordinary graph
limit, replay work account, source depth admission and generated artifacts are
unchanged.

All scratch backing is acquired on the current ordinary ledger before creation
or growth. Geometric growth pays the complete new request with the old backing
still charged. Many-state vectors and their actual Rc headers have separate
acquisitions. Their owning Vec/Weighted Result transports remain charged on
every construction. The explicitly bound nonrecursive invocation prepays its
primitive method frames; those slots grant no allowance and cannot escape the
function, automata and exclusive budget borrow. Function, budget and current
ledger identity plus sticky state authenticate frame use. Sorting is fallible
and stops immediately when a request fails. No source, manifest, certificate,
pointer or cached verdict replaces live language verification.

Owning tests compare the complete existing canonical implementation with the
new proof over genuine compiler types. They cover differently factored common
prefixes, duplicate paths, full Unicode/NUL labels, every byte cutoff through
three complete comparisons, exact/short work, actual requested backing through
Drop, malformed targets/cycles, wide branching, multiplicity overflow, sticky
failure and foreign/restored ledger rejection. Local development checks of the
original JSON decoder and scanner fit their ordinary 64 MiB account. This is
not hosted evidence or a claim that all application fixtures or CI now pass.

Audience: compiler contributors and reviewers.

Status: source-only candidate; no Rust compilation, runtime or cost qualification.

The direct generic copy and immediate-push call is funded by the existing
branch receiving payment before its arguments, formal headers or Vec Result
are formed. Its new bank names all actual/formal argument receivers (including the owned
observation), both Result<Vec<T>, Diagnostic> receivers and the query/control
receivers. The complete earlier inline and TAKE prices remain additional
payments. The callee performs no retrospective receiving debit.

The two-edge first-sibling producer now uses this direct generic call. Clone
work, complete clone-payload census, clone materialization, immediate push work
and materialization retain their order; push work still precedes extra backing,
conversion and physical publication. Checked overflow refusal stays after these
work frontiers. There is no refund, paid-once state, changed cap or credited old
receiver. A selected smaller backing does not establish a net application gain:
all new actual receiver roles remain charged even on fallback.

The current bounded query follows Continue, FunctionReturn and LoopBreak edges
which the shipping enumerator actually traverses. Lexical continuations reserve
an upper bound of one marker; Continue adds none. The walk retains the existing
prefix-length exhaustion bound and admits at most one later fork. Missing
references, a further fork, latch, overflow or exhausted walk decline the shape.
This changes no admitted program, canonical order or work allowance. The generic
fused caller prepays this query separately before entering it. Its old Decision
and TAKE banks remain complete.

frontier tests are unchanged. The complete immediately preceding shipping

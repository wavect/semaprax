# Private ordinary plan history candidate

Base: `c8740045e33126a4375aadd1dc524a25be9245fc`.

The ordinary ActiveBuilder plan enumerator now has a private numeric-link arena.
A branch moves its current tail into an immutable segment once; every child
starts a paid short tail and retains a local segment index. Transition payload
construction and the old skeleton clone/push/queue work and materialization
subsequence remain in the complete producer. New selection, arena and comparison
work is additive ordinary free work. No free-work equality is claimed.

The full original flat plan producer remains unchanged in `replay.rs`, and the
new module's `before.rs` independently compiles its complete producer and old
comparison caller. HIR observations, HIR fusion, renewal, canonical runtime
cleanup vectors, lexical/factored replay, Selected and Oracle remain in their
original representation and are excluded by the new selection predicate.

The comparator constructs the complete plan set first, then sorts it with an
iterative fallible heapsort and two prepaid forward cursors. It compares full
observation values and terminals against the unchanged flat HIR multiset. It
never flattens the shipping plan histories or invents equality after refusal.
Indices point only backward; arena destruction has no recursive parent owners.

`replay.rs.before.txt` is the complete original root. `root-inverse.json` binds
its exact successor and additive inverse. `inputs.json` contains every byte of
all six changed original source inputs, full before/after SHA-256 bindings and
unique inverse edits. The new shared test-only acquisition front restores only
these exact current bytes before the entire unchanged compile35f and older
source custody mappings. Both source inventory joins retain all previous
pieces and add all new children. `manifest.json` binds the new producer and
controls to this private source stage. A later integration must bind its own
whole-source successor before applying this stage's inverses; updating hashes
alone cannot establish that composition.

Authored controls exercise the complete flat predecessor, unchanged old
skeleton/materialization totals, comparison with different segment boundaries
and distinct equal Rc payloads, late trace changes, terminal and multiplicity
changes, first work exhaustion and sticky zero, owner exclusions, malformed
links, exact/one-short/zero whole-operation physical limits and teardown.

`git diff --check`; Python exact reconstruction of all six complete original
inputs from their inverse packets. No Cargo, rustc, test executable, benchmark,
codec/catalog runtime, or full quality profile ran in this lane. Source parsing
and inverse reconstruction do not establish type correctness or runtime parity.

already received budget place; selector/producer/owned-comparator argument,

full-original/inverse/seal recipe; it does not compile or execute the language.

# Ordinary lexical return-operand inventory v1

Status: private source-only checkpoint. Rust compilation, owning tests, Clippy,
source/graph/cache parity and application qualification remain pending. #832 is
OPEN; this change establishes no whole-compiler funding or application recovery.

The paid ordinary lexical caller builds an affine fallible AVL directory over
references to the exact ExpressionIds of independently collected reachable
return operands. It visits facts in their original evaluation order; insertion
keeps full-key ordering and duplicate collapse. No identity or identity backing
is cloned. Node growth uses the existing fallible paid directory, including full
replacement reservation while preceding backing remains charged. No quadratic
scan or later HIR/ExpressionId reconstruction substitutes for membership.

The new inventory's actual initializer/caller, iterator, directory temporary,
error/Result and borrowing controls are paid before capture or construction.
The enclosing driver Result uses its actual generic error type. One declared
unit is charged per inspected lexical fact. Existing directory full-key
comparisons, relocation, node initialization, rebalance and rotation charges
remain separate. Every old verification debit and its ordering is preserved;
new index work is additional. Old/current total work and charged bytes are not
claimed equal, and no cap, refund, discount or quota credit is introduced.

The inventory binds the held function, independent lexical facts, ReplayBudget
address and ClassifierLedger. Each membership or consumer boundary authenticates
those exact owners and makes the existing zero-byte/zero-work sticky requests
before touching backing. A foreign function, facts object, budget, nested owner
or expired owner permanently poisons this receiver, including after restoration.
The index grants no source, certificate, payer or admission authority.

Exit validation uses complete preceding ordinary paid checks and result-source
logic with a fallible authenticated full-key membership seam. Certificate replay
is unchanged and is called only after exit validation and renewed inventory
owner authentication. The inventory remains held through certificate, CFG/path
replay and the final factored comparison, matching the preceding caller's
lifetime. The selected and no-owner drivers retain their entire preceding route.

The complete old lexical caller is retained as a complete independent test module in
`lexical_replay/before_operands.rs` and archived byte-exact. The source packet
reconstructs the ordinary driver and complete exit consumer from archived bodies
with explicit seams, and joins every new source into all three existing source
audits. Tests compare complete old/current success and malformed outcomes with
all six Diagnostic fields, preserve competing early/late diagnostic priority,
and log both absolute declared frontiers outside the observation/account scope.
Separate controls derive actual exact/short/zero index byte/work boundaries,
empty and duplicate frontiers, borrowed identity pointers, spare reuse, sticky
work refusal, foreign/restored/expired ownership, and full acquisition-to-Drop.
All of those Rust controls remain UNEXECUTED.

Qualification must execute the new owners, unchanged retry and normalizer
controls, canonical/source/graph/cache parity and consolidated source custody
at the actual integrated head. Existing other child diagnostics and their
allocation ownership remain outside this caller-inventory cut. #781 remains
OPEN. Evidence and pending selectors live in
